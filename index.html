They tell everyone that their system is based on the heat equations from thermodynamics, but this isn’t the case. They are instead modeling the flow of air over a wing. Instead of heat always flowing from hot to cold, high pressure does not always flow to low pressure. This is the basis of how a wing generates lift by stopping the flow. In their model, the wing is in effect the scenario they are modeling. If there is enough flow then lift can be generated. This lift can be positive or negative depending on the direction they choose. If the flow increases or the scenario “has bumps” then a state of separation occurs. In this state of separation, the scenario acts less as a value play and more as a momentum play. Low flow yields tight correlation with underlying asset values where high flow follows a more technical analysis direction.

Chapter 10

Even with all of the advances in science and technology, humanity has not come much closer to the fundamental questions. Who are we? What are we? How are we?
